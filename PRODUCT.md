# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers, creative developers, founders, and visual makers who collect references from the web and turn them into original, editable websites. They work iteratively across references, prompts, code, images, design systems, and generated outputs, and need a visual workflow that remains understandable as projects grow.

## Product Purpose

Uncraft treats the whole web as a template. It lets users collect pieces from any site, orchestrate those pieces through reusable node workflows, generate or transform websites, and continue refining every result on an infinite canvas. Success means users can reach the product's powerful outcomes without first learning how to invent a graph from scratch, while retaining the canvas as the transparent, editable source of truth.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and resourceful. Uncraft should feel like a trusted working table for creative transformation: familiar enough to use immediately, spatial enough to understand, and capable enough to reward deeper exploration.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble generic AI workflow dashboards, neon-on-black node editors, decorative glassmorphism, marketing-style SaaS card grids, developer-first orchestration tools, or interfaces that hide generative steps behind theatrical progress. Avoid fake affordances, gratuitous motion, ambiguous states, and workflows that force users to understand node architecture before they can get a result.

## Design Principles

1. Show the path to an outcome before asking users to understand the machinery.
2. Keep the canvas as the editable source of truth, even when users enter through guided workflows.
3. Use color, spatial structure, and provenance as functional grammar.
4. Make collected references feel reusable, traceable, and easy to transform.
5. Prefer familiar design-tool conventions and honest system states over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive layouts that keep primary actions reachable without horizontal page scrolling.

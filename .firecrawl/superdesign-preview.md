## Rich Notes Editor

Write, expand, and refine your thinking in a flexible, AI-enhanced editor. Format your content, highlight key insights, and ask AI follow-ups — all in one place.

Edit text styles easily — Make your notes clear and expressive with rich formatting - from bold highlights to code blocks.

Keep notes connected — Link your notes to exact video timestamps or PDF page numbers.

Interactive cards — Instantly add moments from YouTube, and highlights from PDFs and articles, all woven into your notes.

![Edit text styles easily](https://dyau3k4p8o8m9.cloudfront.net/gistr-assets/gistr/featureCaurosels/1.jpg)

## Threads, Collections, Sharing

Structure your learning across sources. Combine insights into threads, organize them, and share your knowledge with the world.

Multiple sources in one thread — Combine insights from YouTube, PDFs, and articles into a single narrative.

Stay organized with collections — Perfect for building your own mini-courses or research hubs.

Share your threads — Publish and share threads with peers, teams, or the public to showcase your learning.

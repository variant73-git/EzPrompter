## What is Whisper AI

Whisper AI is an online speech to text workspace for turning spoken content into editable, searchable, and export-ready text. Upload audio or video, record in the browser, or import a media URL, then use AI transcription to create meeting notes, interview transcripts, podcast drafts, captions, and audio to text archives.

### Speech to Text Workspace

Bring upload, recording, language settings, transcript review, search, editing, and export into one focused Whisper AI workflow.

### Built for Real Recordings

Convert meetings, interviews, podcasts, lectures, webinars, support calls, and video voice tracks into usable text.

### Export-Ready Transcripts

Use the finished transcript for notes, captions, summaries, articles, documentation, archives, and downstream AI workflows.

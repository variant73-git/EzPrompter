**Amici** is the sole reference. It provides a restaurant-specific vertical spine: statement-led hero, appetite collage, concise philosophy, locations, and reservation close. The reference has four primary sections, so its length does not override DaSelva's frozen seven-movement content envelope.

Hotel commerce, room hierarchy, and virtual scrolling are excluded. Museum archive, interview, font-catalog, newsletter, and search mechanics are excluded. Donors may not introduce a second global grid, palette, navigation, or scroll authority.

Before W code exists, it must produce one DaSelva grammar, desktop and mobile composition boards, and a temporal storyboard with reduced-motion and mobile fallbacks.

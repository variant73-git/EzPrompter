# DaSelva three-strategy experiment — Gate A

This directory freezes the shared target fixture for the S/C/W real-output comparison described in `docs/superpowers/handoffs/2026-08-02-start-from-ref-three-strategy-real-output-experiment.md`.

## Frozen editorial decision

The source document contains more material than a focused landing page can carry. The home is therefore frozen as seven movements:

1. Hero: category, city, two houses, reservation.
2. Banda: the signature shared-table ritual, within the first 35% of scroll.
3. Dieta Amazônica: one concise thesis, not the full manifesto.
4. Ingredients and menu: appetite and breadth without an invented full menu.
5. Origin: traceability without unconfirmed community naming or technical promises.
6. Houses and reservations: neighborhoods and reservation intent only.
7. Institutional signature.

`/banda-de-tambaqui` carries the longer ritual, fish, cut, weekend and search-intent content. The home and dedicated route are the only required routes. Cardápio, origem and reservas remain home anchors because no current full menu, operational booking destination or validated contact details were supplied.

The experiment uses the pre-INPI A language. The protected product-style name is not allowed. The “se não gostar, não paga” challenge, Tamanicuá name, exact street addresses, hours, phone numbers, WhatsApp links, live booking links, full menu and prices are excluded from visible output until their own source conditions are resolved.

## Frozen fairness decision

All strategies receive the same target truth, palette, content-capacity envelope, media pack and model budget. Only their reference strategy and construction process differ. The target-media pack does not exist yet; Gate C must create or receive it once, freeze its hash, and make the identical files available to all strategies before any strategy-specific model call.

The cost ceiling follows the repository's current estimates: 475 credits per strategy plus 120 credits for up to six shared Gemini image generations, totaling 1,545 credits or USD 15.45 equivalent. This budget is a cap, not spending approval.

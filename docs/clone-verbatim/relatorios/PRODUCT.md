# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Progressive growers, agronomy teams, and agricultural operators evaluating new soil-intelligence tools. They arrive curious but skeptical and need to understand the product, the operating principle, and the field-level payoff quickly.

## Product Purpose

FLUX introduces Pulse One, a compact field sensor that reads the soil’s biological and water signals and turns them into precise timing recommendations. The landing page should make an unfamiliar physical product feel inevitable, credible, and desirable.

## Brand Personality

Engineered, buoyant, luminous. The voice is concise and confident, with a sense of controlled optimism rather than agricultural nostalgia.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic green sustainability branding, corporate agritech dashboards, soft beige SaaS layouts, stock-card feature grids, decorative gradients on text, and rustic farm visual clichés.

## Design Principles

1. Let the physical product lead the story.
2. Explain one idea per viewport.
3. Make motion reveal causality, not decoration.
4. Pair evidence with emotional scale.
5. Keep every interaction immediate and legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, preserve keyboard focus, use semantic landmarks, provide useful media alternatives, and reduce transform-heavy choreography when reduced motion is requested.
